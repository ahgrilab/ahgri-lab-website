A Durable Way to Use Any Tool

The 3-check method

Irrigation decisions are stronger when climate estimates, soil information and field observations tell a consistent story. When the three checks do not align, review the inputs, measurements and field conditions before changing the schedule.

01

Climate estimates demand

Use reference ET with an appropriate crop coefficient to estimate crop water use. Satellite-based actual ET can help review field-scale patterns over time.

02

Soil defines storage

Account for available water capacity, effective rooting depth and the allowable depletion that fits the crop and growth stage.

03

The field verifies reality

Check soil moisture, rooting, crop condition, applied depth and distribution uniformity. Correct the model when the field disagrees.
