Irrigation Measurement Calculators

Measure water. Manage every drop.

Turn field observations into irrigation-ready numbers. Choose a method, enter your measurements, and see the result with the formula behind it. A tool of the AHGRI Lab at Oregon State University.

01

Timed-volume discharge

Collect water in a container of known volume and time each fill. Enter one fill time per trial; the calculator averages the trial discharges.

FormulaQ = volume ÷ time, averaged across trials
02

Applied irrigation depth

Convert system flow and runtime to an average depth over the irrigated area.

Methoddepth = applied volume ÷ irrigated area
03

Catch-can uniformity

Paste catch depths to calculate low-quarter distribution uniformity and Christiansen CU.

FormulaDUlq = low-quarter mean ÷ overall mean
04

Root-zone soil-water deficit

Estimate the refill needed to return the active root zone to field capacity.

Formuladeficit = (θfc − θv) × root depth

Field Guide

Measure with a method, not a guess

Good calculations begin with representative observations. Use these short protocols to prepare, measure, and document each field test.

Measurement Quality

Three habits improve every result

01

Repeat

Collect replicates across representative operating conditions, not just the easiest point to access.

02

Record

Note pressure, wind, runtime, set area, sensor depth, and unusual field conditions with the measurement.

03

Compare

Compare measured performance with design values, prior tests, and crop or soil-water requirements.
